Why Study in Malaysia?

Asia's emerging education hub offering world-class degrees at affordable costs.

Affordable World-Class Education

Out of 64 universities, more than 24 are ranked in the QS World Top 1000, offering globally recognised degrees at a fraction of Western tuition costs.

UNESCO’s 9th Most Preferred Study Destination

Malaysia continues to be one of the world’s top choices for international students.

Global Branch Campuses

13 foreign universities from countries like the UK, Australia, Ireland, China, and India have established their branch campuses in Malaysia.

Top Student City

Kuala Lumpur is ranked No. 2 in the QS Best Student Cities 2025, reflecting its quality of life, affordability, and student-friendly environment.

Truly International Community

With nearly 250,000 international students from 130+ countries, Malaysian universities offer a multicultural and globally connected learning experience.
